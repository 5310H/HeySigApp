# Noise quick start

1. Open Noise → Choose programs. Assign Universal, Noisy and Very Noisy, then save.
2. Tap Off to start Automatic and read the microphone explanation. Green On and notification Off stop it.
3. Automatic uses ten-second samples with three noise sections. Partly masked speech leads to Noisy; heavier masking can lead to Very Noisy. Clear or confirmed quiet leads back to Universal.
4. Allow repeated checks and the saved cooldown. Unsure means hold the current program.
5. For diagnosis, stop Automatic and use Estimate noise at your normal listening position. It is a foreground-only check and sends no hearing-aid command.
6. To remember a mistake, open Teach Automatic within two minutes, review the right noise category and tap Remember. Disable or forget memories if needed.

Automatic does not select TV based on sound. Existing TV choices remain saved for compatibility. The phone estimates speech masking; it does not read the hearing aids' internal noise cancellation. See [expected behavior and limitations](how-automatic-noise-works.md). Manual control and Therapy stop Automatic; calibration and memories are optional.
