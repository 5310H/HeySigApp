# Try the speech and noise check

This shows what HeySig thinks it hears. **It does not change your hearing aids.**

1. Open **Noise → Check speech and noise**. Automatic stops for this try-out.
2. Tap the red **Off** button to start. Read the microphone explanation and
   choose **Continue** if you want to try it.
3. Keep this screen open and the phone unlocked. Point the phone toward the
   sound you want to check. Allow about 15 seconds for two matching checks.
4. Look for **Mostly speech**, **Speech and noise**, or **Mostly noise**.
   **Not sure yet** is also a normal answer, especially in quiet or with music.
5. Tap the green **On** button to stop. Leaving the screen or locking the phone
   also stops it. It stops by itself after five minutes.

Try ordinary conversation, conversation with everyday background sound, and an
ordinary noise such as a fan. Keep sounds comfortable; do not create loud noise.
Compare the description with what is actually happening around you.

This is a first try-out, not a reliable judge of speech clarity. It cannot know
which person you want to hear or whether you understand their words. A TV voice
may also count as speech. No program is chosen from these descriptions.

Sound is processed on the phone, then discarded. The check does not save or
upload recordings, words or an answer history. Only the choice to allow this
microphone feature is saved. Voice commands are separate.

If you want ordinary Automatic program changes afterward, go back to **Noise**
and turn Automatic on yourself. Your program choices and fitting stay the same.
